The fine print

Terms & Privacy

Plain-language rules for shopping and riding with PelotonHub.

Terms of Service

  1. Reservations, not online payment. Reserving an item holds it for 48 hours. You pay at the shop counter when you pick it up. Unclaimed reservations are released after 48 hours.
  2. Prices and stock. Prices are in Philippine pesos. Stock shown online is updated live but can sell out; if an item is no longer available we will tell you before pickup.
  3. Membership. Every account starts on Spoke (free). Chain (₱499/month) and Peloton (₱999/month) are paid online or at the shop counter and switched on once the payment is confirmed. Member discounts apply to reservations made while your tier is active; promo codes do not stack with member discounts — the larger discount applies.
  4. Cancelling. You can cancel a reservation any time before pickup from My Orders.
  5. Events. Group rides and charity events are at your own risk; wear a helmet and follow the ride leader. RSVPs can be cancelled from the Events page.
  6. Reviews. Reviews must be honest and about the product. We may remove reviews that are abusive or off-topic.

Privacy Policy

  1. What we keep: your name, email, mobile number, optional address, membership tier, reservations, wishlist, RSVPs and support messages. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from them — never your password or posts.
  2. Why: to hold your reservations, apply your member discount, reply to your messages and run events. We do not sell your data.
  3. Who sees it: PelotonHub staff, through the shop’s admin console. Data is stored with Google Firebase.
  4. Your choices: update your details on My Profile, or ask us through Support to delete your account.

Delete Your Account & Data

  1. How to ask: send a message through Support with the topic “Other” and the words “Delete my account”, using the email address on your account. You can also ask at the shop counter.
  2. What we delete: your account and sign-in, profile details, wishlist, cart, RSVPs and support messages. Completed purchase records the shop must keep for accounting are kept without your name and contact details.
  3. When: within 7 days. We email you when it’s done.
  4. Signed in with Facebook or Google? You can also remove PelotonHub from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, or from your Google account under Security → Third-party connections. That stops future sign-ins; to delete the data we already hold, send the request above.
